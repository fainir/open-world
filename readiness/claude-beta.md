## Limits, costs and authority

The profile accepts at most 768 KiB of decoded text and of the entire serialized signed envelope, and at most 8,000 output tokens. Actor allowance is two and the independent network allowance four per rolling 24 hours; app budget is $25 per rolling 30 days. Initial central serving budget is $100 plus a $100 external baseline within the $200 outer ceiling. Allocations may total $150 but do not raise the active shared ceiling. The coordinator's activation expires November 3 at 00:00 UTC. Shared capacity can refuse requests; it is not a user wallet or a fixed-price-per-creation promise.

Actors come from authenticated accounts or existing cryptographic guest capabilities; the first bounded creation requires no signup. Server HMAC actor/network digests are sent, never raw account IDs, guest proofs or IPs. The network identity uses canonical Railway ingress X-Real-IP with validation and IPv6 /64 grouping. Internal/noncanonical hosts cannot fund creations. Signing uses a purpose-separated Ed25519 seed derived from the existing JWT_SECRET; only its public SPKI is retained in qa/claude-beta-public-key.txt.

SQLite atomically binds the request UUID, actor and payload hash, reserves the local allowance, and replays completed private versions. Only explicit certain pre-dispatch proof releases actor allowance; unknown or contradictory outcomes retain it. The network submitted-attempt ceiling still applies. Funded work owns its session and completes independently of browser waiting; a closed private session cannot be recreated by a late result. No automatic new-ID retry or direct provider fallback is allowed.

The gateway retains budget/idempotency metadata, not raw prompts in its database/logs. Successful result text is encrypted for recovery for up to one hour; ciphertext cleanup is opportunistic. This is access expiry, not cryptographic erasure or a backup-deletion guarantee. Prompts and source are processed by the gateway and Anthropic as disclosed. Existing export/deletion/contact behavior and optional BYOK's image/model/16,000-output route remain unchanged.
