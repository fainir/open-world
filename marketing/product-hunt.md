## Description

Open World is a 3D open-world game that runs in your browser — on desktop and mobile.

But it's not just a game. It's a game *creator*.

Click the edit button, describe any change in plain English, and AI rewrites the game code in real time. Add flying cars, neon cities, day/night cycles, racing modes, weather systems — anything you can describe.

**How it works:**
1. Open the game in your browser
2. Click the AI edit button
3. Describe what you want ("add a minimap", "make it snow", "create a racing track")
4. AI reads the entire codebase and makes targeted code edits
5. Play your new version instantly

**Every version gets a shareable URL.** Build your dream game and send the link to friends.

**The game includes:**
- Open world with city, beach, mountains, desert, harbor, forest
- Vehicles (cars, bikes), tricks system, combat, wingsuit gliding
- Interiors: bars, shops, restaurants, sports venues
- Full mobile support with touch controls

**Built with:** Three.js, Claude, FastAPI

No downloads. No coding. Just describe it.

## First Comment
Hey Product Hunt! I'm the maker of Open World.

The idea started simple: what if players could modify the game they're playing just by describing what they want?

The AI reads the entire game codebase (~5000 lines of Three.js), understands the architecture, and makes surgical code edits. It's not generating a new game from scratch — it's intelligently modifying an existing, playable open world.

The vision is a community where everyone creates and shares their own version of the game. Your imagination is the only limit.
